Method & limits

STRATOS is a thinking aid. It helps structure uncertainty; it does not remove it.

What the engine does

  • Collects variables with direction, importance, current level, and confidence.
  • Applies scenario adjustments to explore alternative conditions.
  • Calculates a weighted heuristic exposure score.
  • Uses declared relationships to flag possible knock-on effects.
  • Generates stress-test prompts and experiment ideas.

What the engine does not do

  • It does not know the true state of your market or relationships.
  • It does not estimate validated probabilities or guarantee outcomes.
  • It does not infer culture, intent, or human behaviour from stereotypes.
  • It does not establish causation from a relationship you enter.
  • It cannot replace domain expertise, legal advice, or direct observation.

How to use scores

Treat scores as prompts for comparison, not facts. A score is only as useful as the variables, scales, and evidence behind it. Change one assumption at a time and see whether the decision changes.

Good modelling practice

  • Use observable variables rather than vague labels.
  • Mark assumptions and unknowns explicitly.
  • Include constraints and opposing forces.
  • Seek disconfirming evidence, not only confirmation.
  • Revise the model when reality disagrees.
